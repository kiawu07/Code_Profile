This project is a bank app system simulator(for investing)
Broad ideas is should:
    Open an account for a client
    Enable deposits and withdrawals
    Investment simulator

    Add ons:
    Have a seperate online shopping part
    Have a part to pay for online services


Open an Account for client:
    Inputs personal info
    Generates an account number
    User input password

Enable deposits and withdrawals:
    Checks deposit credentials
    Check withdrawal credentials
    Make proper adjustments to account
    Withdrawal based on account type chosen


Investment simulator
    User choose investment account type
    User choose stock to invest in
    Simulate stock growth or loss
